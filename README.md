# Gary Oberbrunner

Mostly-retired Emmy Award-winning technology executive and high-performance software developer. 30+ years building graphics, visual effects, and scientific visualization tools. Since retirement, I'm busier than ever, creating open source tools and projects, and leading [Long Now Boston](https://longnowboston.org).

I still take on some VFX, graphics and fractional-CTO consulting through [Dark Star Systems](https://darkstarsystems.com).

Previously CTO of GenArts (acquired by Boris FX), where I led 20+ years of visual-effects software development. 

[MIT '84](https://web.mit.edu/).

## Selected projects

Since "retirement" I've been working through my life-long backlog of "wouldn't it be great if someone wrote..." projects, most with some AI collaboration. See my [blog](https://oberbrunner.com) for my conflicted feelings about what the oncoming AI freight train will do for or to humanity. Here are some projects I've done recently and am still working on:

- **[pcons](https://github.com/DarkStarSystems/pcons)** — modern Python-based software construction tool; inspired by SCons and CMake.
- **[deep-time-timeline](https://github.com/garyo/deep-time-timeline)** — log-scaled visualization of world events, to help us situate our own short lives in the continuous stream of deep time. Live: [deep-timeline.org](https://deep-timeline.org/)
- **[pelorus-nav](https://github.com/garyo/pelorus-nav)** — open-source web-based marine chartplotter. Try it at [pelorus-nav](https://pelorus-nav.com)
- **[jazz-calendar](https://github.com/garyo/jazz-calendar)** — every jazz show in Greater Boston, in one place: [bostonjazzcalendar.com](https://bostonjazzcalendar.com)
- **[packzen](https://github.com/garyo/packzen)** — packing list app for travelers ([packzen.org](https://packzen.org))
- **[featherstat](https://github.com/garyo/featherstat)** — fast, small, self-hosted multi-site web analytics
- **[gco-llm-pkm](https://github.com/garyo/gco-llm-pkm)** — LLM-based personal knowledge management system
- **[sea-surface-temp-viz](https://github.com/garyo/sea-surface-temp-viz)** — daily sea-surface-temperature anomaly visualization, with [3d globe](https://globe-viz.oberbrunner.com)
- **[emacs-config](https://github.com/garyo/emacs-config)** — my Emacs config, refined over decades
